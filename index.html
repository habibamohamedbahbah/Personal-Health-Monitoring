SignFlow

Sign Language Interpretation System

This system offers innovative AI-powered solutions that convert sign language into text or speech in real time. It enhances communication between individuals with hearing impairments and their caregivers or surrounding community, ensuring a smoother and more independent communication experience.

Accurate Interpretation

• Advanced hand-tracking and facial recognition for precise understanding of sign language. Converts gestures into clear, comprehensible words for effective daily communication.

Restorative Communication

• Rebuilds communication bridges in healthcare, education, and other environments. Minimizes errors and misunderstandings, promoting inclusivity and reliable linguistic exchange.

Personalized Assistance

• Adapts to each user's unique signing style, learning individual movement patterns over time. Delivers a precise, personalized experience to enhance comprehension and support communication in various contexts.
